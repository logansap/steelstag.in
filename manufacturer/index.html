Welcome

For approved manufacturing partners · Last updated June 2026

SteelStag is a premium solid-color t-shirt brand built on one idea: that a perfectly constructed tee in a considered color is all you need. No graphics, no prints — just fabric, form, and color done right.

This portal contains everything your production team needs to manufacture SteelStag garments to specification. All documents are versioned. If you have a question not answered here, contact the buyer directly using the details in the Contact section.

Precision over speed

We would rather wait for a correct sample than approve a fast one. Quality checkpoints are non-negotiable.

Fabric first

125 GSM combed cotton is the foundation. Mill test reports are required with every sample shipment.

Color consistency

Reactive dyeing with Pantone TCX references. Lab dip approval before any cutting begins — no exceptions.

No shortcuts on finish

Clean-bound neck with no rib band. Self-fabric tape, flat topstitch. This is the defining detail of the garment.

Manufacturing Philosophy

What we expect from every production partner

SteelStag garments are positioned as premium basics. The construction is intentionally simple — but that simplicity demands execution that is exact. A standard tee factory can make these garments; a good one can make them well.

We work with factories that treat the tech pack as a floor, not a ceiling. If you see something in our specification that you believe can be improved, tell us. We welcome that input before sampling, not after bulk is cut.

Important: Do not substitute any material, trim, or construction detail without written approval from the buyer. Unauthorized substitutions will result in rejection of the entire shipment.

Production Workflow

From enquiry to delivery — 8 stages
1

RFQ & Costing Both

Factory submits costing sheet broken down by fabric, CMT, trims, and packaging. Buyer reviews and confirms order placement.

2

Lab Dip Approval Buyer Approval

All 5 colors dyed on approved 125 GSM combed cotton. Shade band submitted with lab dips. Written approval required before any fabric is committed to bulk dyeing.

3

Fit Sample Buyer Approval

1 pc per style in Size M, Seaport Teal or Bright White. Shipped to buyer address with measurement worksheet. Buyer provides sign-off within 5 working days of receipt.

4

Pre-Production Sample Buyer Approval

1 pc per style × all 5 colors, incorporating all fit comments. Final approval before cutting begins.

5

Bulk Production Factory

200 pcs per color, 2 styles (100 pcs each). Size split: S 15% / M 35% / L 35% / XL 15%. AQL 2.5 inline inspection required.

6

Top of Production Both

2 pcs per style × per color from first bulk roll submitted for buyer retention as bulk standard.

7

Final Inspection Factory

AQL 2.5 final inspection. Measurement audit on 10% of units per size. Shade band check across all cartons.

8

Shipment & Documentation Factory

Packed per spec (see Packaging section). Commercial invoice, packing list, and GST/LUT documentation provided. AWB shared on dispatch.

FAQ

Common questions from manufacturing partners
What is the minimum order quantity per color?
200 pcs per color (100 pcs per style × 2 styles). This is set at 200 to ensure dye lot consistency — reactive dyeing below this quantity risks shade variation between pieces.
Can we substitute the rib neck finish for a clean-bound finish?
No. The clean-bound self-fabric neck with no rib band is a defining spec. All 3 neck styles use this construction. Reference the TechPack for exact topstitch placement.
Where do we ship samples?
All samples ship to the buyer in Hungary via DHL or FedEx express. Shipping address and contact in the Contact section. Share the AWB number by email on the day of dispatch.
Can we source the kraft pouch locally?
Yes, but the spec must match exactly: 32×40cm, matte kraft laminate, zipper seal, two-tone print. Send a sample pouch for buyer approval before ordering full quantity.
What certifications are required?
OCS or BCI certificate for cotton is preferred but not mandatory for SS2026. A mill test report (GSM, shrinkage, color fastness) is required with every sample shipment.
How long does buyer approval take after receiving samples?
5 working days from confirmed receipt. If approval is delayed beyond this, the buyer will communicate a revised date. Do not proceed to the next stage without written approval.
