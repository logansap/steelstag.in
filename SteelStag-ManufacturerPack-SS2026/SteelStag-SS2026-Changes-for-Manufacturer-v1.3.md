# SteelStag SS2026 — What changed in v1.3

**Version 1.3 · 11 September 2026**
Contact: hello@steelstag.in

---

## Read this first

**v1.3 changes documentation, artwork and packaging only.**
**No garment fit, sizing, grading, measurement value, tolerance or construction detail has changed.**
**Garments already in production are unaffected and require no rework.**

Bulk production is running against the Tech Pack plus the agreed factory pattern
and production reference confirmed directly with SteelStag. v1.3 does not alter
any of that. It corrects the written package and the print artwork so that
everything says the same thing.

Use the v1.3 files. Discard earlier copies.

## What changed that affects you

### Garment
Nothing. One confirmation only — see "What we need from you" below.

### Labels, hang tags and stickers — **reprint from v1.3 files**
- **Finished sizes are now published** for every item (they were missing before).
- **Bleed rule corrected.** Bleed and crop marks are supplied only where the
  item is physically trimmed or die-cut. The wash care label, hang tags and
  size stickers carry 3 mm bleed on every edge. The **neck label does not** —
  it is applied to the garment, nothing is trimmed, so its file is the
  50 × 30 mm artwork area exactly, with no bleed and no crop marks.
- **Hang tags and size stickers now actually bleed.** Previously the ink
  stopped at the trim line, which would have left a white edge on the dark
  tags after cutting.
- **Care symbols corrected.** The tumble-dry symbol was drawn wrong and the
  iron symbol contradicted the printed text. Both artworks now carry an
  identical, self-consistent care set.
- **Neck label artwork was being clipped** by its own boundary. Fixed.
- **File resolution honestly named.** Files previously called `-300dpi` were
  actually 384 DPI and carried no DPI metadata — placing them at 300 DPI
  would have printed every label 28% oversize. They are now named `-384dpi`
  and carry correct metadata. Prefer the print-ready PDF, which is sized
  correctly regardless of placement settings.

### Packaging
- **Folding instruction corrected.** It still referenced the superseded
  32 × 40 cm pouch. Now: manufacturer-standard retail fold to suit the
  approved 24 × 34 cm pouch.
- **Two pouch artboards** — front and back. Front is brand only. The back
  carries no printed declarations.
- **Declarations move to an applied sticker.** A 70 × 38 mm self-adhesive
  retail declaration sticker is applied to the back of the pouch at packing.
  The pouch itself holds no MRP, SKU, date or barcode, so pouch printing does
  not depend on those values.
- **Master carton** — max 100 pcs or 18 kg gross, whichever comes first.
  Packing per manufacturer standard export packing, maintaining SKU-wise
  quantities and a carton-wise packing list.

### Colour
- **Pantone TCX numbers are now printed as text** in the Tech Pack. Previously
  they existed only inside small images and were not reliably legible.
  The numbers themselves are unchanged — any lab dip already submitted or
  agreed is unaffected.

### Logo
- The approved visual master is `SteelStag-Logo-Transparent.png`. All artwork
  must visually match it — no recolouring, no tinting, no alternate spacing.
- `SteelStag-Logo.ai` is now supplied.
- The `.svg` / `.pdf` / `.ai` files are production derivatives: same geometry
  and spacing, simplified metallic treatment. Use them where vector is
  genuinely required. Do not use them in place of the master in full-colour
  applications.

### Sampling
- **No TOP submission required.** SS2026 does not require top-of-production
  samples. Quality is confirmed by final random production QC — random
  sampling across finished production to AQL 2.5, plus measurement audit and
  shade check.

## What you should do

1. **Use v1.3 artwork only.** Destroy or archive any earlier label, tag,
   sticker or pouch files.
2. **Confirm the measurement tolerance in use is ±¼"** (0.25"). The Tech Pack
   has said ±¼" since v1.0; only an older README copy said ±0.5". If any part
   of the run was graded or inspected to ±0.5", tell us before final
   inspection.
3. **Send us the sleeve length** measured on the current production reference,
   so it can be written into the spec.
4. **Confirm the manufactured-and-packed-by wording** for the retail sticker,
   in writing:
   - your legal entity name exactly as registered;
   - which premises actually manufactures and packs this order;
   - the full address wording as it appears on your GST registration;
   - whether the same entity both manufactures and packs.

## What is on hold, and why

| Item | Status |
|---|---|
| **Retail declaration stickers** | **Do not print.** SteelStag must issue the MRP, the 40-SKU code list and the manufactured-and-packed-by wording first. Do not substitute your own values. |
| **Bright White neck labels** | **Hold.** One metallic artwork covers all five colourways. It reads correctly on the four dark grounds. For Bright White, print or apply a physical sample on the approved fabric and send it to us for approval before running the rest. |
| **Pouch printing** | Not blocked by the above — the pouch carries no variable data. The dieline is produced by your converter to manufacturer standard from the 24 × 34 cm size and the two-tone layout in the Tech Pack. |
| **Garment production** | **Not blocked.** Continue. |
